How the projection works

Opportunities × efficiency. His pass attempts, carries or targets per game, with recent games counting more (a game four weeks back counts half as much as last week), nudged by how many this defense allows to his position and his team's implied points, plus work opened up by teammates ruled out. Then times his yards per attempt, carry or target (or catch rate), recent games weighted more and steadied toward the league average.

What five seasons taught it. Recent form matters more than the season average. Matchups matter less than they look: the defense's effect on volume is applied at a quarter strength, and its yards-per-play effect and game script didn't help at all, so they're out.

The spread. His own game-to-game swings, steadied with a typical swing for the stat. Rushing, receiving and catches use a lopsided curve, because most games land below a player's average and a few big ones pull it up. Passing yards use a normal bell curve.
